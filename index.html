Our Story

Wilder Granola started in our DC kitchen, with a sheet pan, a big bag of oats, and a habit of giving jars away to anyone who stopped by.

Friends kept asking for refills, so we kept baking. Every batch is still mixed, toasted, and packed by hand, with gluten-free oats, real maple syrup, good olive oil, and nothing you can't pronounce.

We think breakfast should be a little wild. We hope you do too.

— The Wilder crew
